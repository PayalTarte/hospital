Cardiac Sciences FAQ's

-If you live an unhealthy lifestyle or have a genetic risk of heart issues, your general physician may send you to a cardiologist. -Red flags like high cholesterol and blood pressure may indicate heart problems. -Cardiologists specialize in treating heart, artery, and vein issues..

- Cardiology deals with heart and blood vessel disorders. - Common tests include ECG to check heartbeat, ambulatory ECG during physical activities, stress test for heart activity, and echocardiogram for heart structure. - These tests help diagnose and treat heart conditions.

- After a referral, a cardiologist manages your heart care for the long term. - The first visit includes discussing overall condition, test results, medical history, lifestyle, and health changes over time. - Ensures you receive the right treatment based on diagnosis.

- Consider a cardiologist and annual check-up in middle age. - Necessarily if at risk of heart failure, chest pains (Angina), or have risk factors like high cholesterol, blood sugar, and blood pressure..

- Cardiologists are not surgeons; surgery is the job of cardiac surgeons. - Invasive cardiology involves procedures such as cardiac catheterizations, but not surgery. - Cardiologists may refer you to a cardiothoracic surgeon for invasive surgery..
